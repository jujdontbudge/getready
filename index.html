How it works

Pick it. Run it. Prove it.

No streaks, no shaming, no history to obsess over. A voice does the executive-function work, and a photo proves you actually finished.

Pick Choose a routine Build named routines — Morning Full, Quick Rinse, Night — each an ordered list of phases with your own durations. Tap one and start.
Run The phone talks you through A real voice calls out each phase right on the clock — no touching a screen with wet hands. Finished a phase early? One big button skips ahead.
Prove A photo finishes it The routine only completes when you snap a live photo of yourself dressed and an on-device check confirms it. Old pictures don't count — and the photo never leaves your phone.
Coming: Focus Lock.

An optional shield that blocks your own distracting apps while a routine is running, so the phone can't eat the morning — built on Apple's Screen Time technology, and unlocked the moment you finish. You choose which apps; it only ever manages your own device.

Privacy

The photo never leaves your phone.

Get Ready checks that you're dressed using an on-device model. There's no account and no photo upload — the completion picture is analyzed locally and stays on your device.

Photos stay on-device

The completion photo is captured live in the app, checked by an on-device model, and never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared.

No account, no profile

There's nothing to sign up for. Your routines live on your device. Get Ready doesn't build a profile of you or your habits.

Voice made ahead of time

Callouts are generated when you set up a routine, then played from your device. A run never needs the network — only writing a custom line does.

Focus Lock sees opaque tokens

When Focus Lock arrives, the apps you block are opaque tokens — Get Ready can't read or learn which apps they are. It only manages your own device.
