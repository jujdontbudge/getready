Privacy Policy

Last updated · July 2026

Get Ready is an independent iOS app developed by Johan Sardar. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches and where it lives. The short version: your completion photos and your routines stay on your device.

No account. No photo upload. Get Ready does not require you to sign up, does not upload your completion photos, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that profile you.

1. Completion photos (camera)

To finish a routine, Get Ready asks you to take a live photo of yourself dressed and checks it with an on-device model to confirm a dressed person is in frame.

  • The photo is captured live in the app and analyzed entirely on your device.
  • It is never uploaded, transmitted, sold, or shared. It does not leave your phone.
  • You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

2. Your routines & settings

The routines you create, their phases, durations, and your settings are stored locally on your device. Get Ready has no user accounts and no backend server for your content.

3. Voice callouts (ElevenLabs)

Stock voice lines are generated ahead of time and shipped with the app. If you write a custom callout line, the text you type is sent to ElevenLabs to generate the audio in the voice you chose; the resulting audio is then cached on your device and played from there.

  • Only the custom line text you author is sent, only at the moment you create it, only to generate that audio.
  • Running a routine never needs the network — audio plays from your device.

4. Screen Time data (Focus Lock)

Get Ready's optional Focus Lock feature uses Apple's native Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to let you block your own chosen apps while a routine is running.

  • The apps you select are represented to Get Ready as opaque tokens. We cannot see, read, decode, or reconstruct which apps they are.
  • These tokens and your selections are stored locally on device in a secure shared App Group container so the app and its system extensions can coordinate. None of it is transmitted off the device.
  • Focus Lock manages restrictions on your own device only. Get Ready is not a parental-control product and uses individual Screen Time authorization, which you can revoke in Settings at any time.

5. Subscriptions

Get Ready offers a paid subscription. Payment is handled by Apple, and subscription status is managed through a subscription provider (RevenueCat). Apple processes the transaction; Get Ready does not receive or store your payment-card details.

6. What we do NOT do

  • We do not upload or store your completion photos anywhere off your device.
  • We do not build a profile of your app usage or browsing history.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Children

Get Ready is designed for adults managing their own device and, for Focus Lock, uses individual Screen Time authorization. It is not a parental-control product.
